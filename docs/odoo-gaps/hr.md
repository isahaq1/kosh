# HR & people: Odoo 19 Community vs Kosh — feature gaps

Scope. Odoo: `hr`, `hr_holidays`, `hr_attendance`, `hr_expense`, `hr_recruitment` (+ `_skills`, `_survey`, `_sms`), `hr_skills` (+ `_survey`, `_slides`, `_event`), `hr_work_entry` (+ `_holidays`), `hr_homeworking` (+ `_calendar`), `hr_holidays_homeworking`, `hr_holidays_attendance`, `hr_presence`, `hr_fleet`, `hr_gamification`, `hr_org_chart`, `hr_timesheet`, `hr_hourly_cost`, `hr_maintenance`, `hr_calendar`, `resource`, `fleet`, `lunch`, `gamification`.
Kosh: `modules/hr` (Workforce: `hr.department`, `hr.job`, `hr.employee`, `hr.contract`, `hr.leave.type`, `hr.leave`), `recruitment` (Hiring: `hiring.stage/opening/applicant/scorecard`), `attendance`, `payroll`, `payroll_account`, `l10n_bd_payroll`, `expense` (Spend), `expense_account`, `calendar` (Agenda), plus HR-adjacent bits in `project` (hourly costs, timesheets, workload) and `approvals`.

## 1. Kosh already ahead

- **Payroll exists at all**: Odoo Community has no payroll. Kosh has a safe formula language, parameters and slab tables as data, runs with explained payslips, corrections, loans and repayments, year-to-date figures, bank and bKash / Nagad files, posting to Accounting (`payroll_account`), and Bangladesh rules (`l10n_bd_payroll`: garments structure, festival bonus, provident fund, tax at source from the projected year).
- **Shifts and rosters**: `attendance.shift` / `attendance.roster` with grace, break, night shifts and overtime in steps at a rate. Odoo Community has no shift planning (Planning is Enterprise).
- **Attendance capture**: geofenced check-in by phone (`attendance.site` with radius, `inside`, `distance`), a kiosk by badge or PIN, ZKTeco / CSV device import. Each person gets a computed **attendance day** (late, early, absent, leave, holiday, overtime) that HR approves before Payroll reads it. Odoo's closest equivalent is work entries plus overtime.
- **Spend policies**: per-category limits per expense and per month, receipts required above an amount, the reason for going over policy demanded at submission with an extra approval level, duplicates flagged, per-diem and mileage rates. **Cash advances** are settled by expense reports, which Odoo does not have.
- **Hiring fairness**: interviewers fill independent scorecards (each sees only their own) and the applicant shows the average rating. One application per email per opening. Hiring is one step into Workforce and closes the filled opening. Expected salary is restricted by field security.
- **Leave integrity**: nobody approves their own leave, not even an HR manager. Overlapping requests are blocked. The server checks the working days against the company's week.
- **Security**: personal fields (national ID, home address, birthday, wage) are visible to HR managers only, through field security. Record rules give own, team (line manager) and all. Everything is audited.
- **Agenda**: rooms can never be double-booked, private events show as busy, free / busy lookup, and today's meetings appear on the home page.
- **The org chart** is a hierarchy view. Project hourly costs, team allocation and the Workload heat map (RFC 0026) cover `hr_hourly_cost` and the HR side of `hr_timesheet`.
- **Platform**: a generic approvals engine with quorum, delegation, deadlines and decisions from email; Documents and Sign; Bangla throughout.

## 3. Recommended next steps (top 8 by value)

1. **Leave allocations and balances (Workforce step 2).** Add `hr.leave.allocation`: granted by HR, requested by the person, or generated in bulk by company, department or tag, with validity dates. Show the live balance on the form, on the employee and on a "my time off" dashboard. Count public holidays (move them to Workforce, or read `attendance.holiday`) and support paid, unpaid and worked-time types that feed payroll's `LEAVE_DAYS` separately.
2. **Accrual plans with Bangladesh rules as data.** Accrual levels by seniority, frequency, caps and carry-over, run by a job, each accrual explained in words, as payroll lines are. Ship `l10n_bd` presets (casual 10, sick 14, earned 1 per 18 worked days from approved attendance days, maternity 16 weeks) so earned leave follows actual attendance, which Odoo cannot do.
3. **Half-day and hourly leave, per-type validation and cancellation.** Track leave in exact units (days or hours against the person's shift). Route approval through the approvals engine (none / manager / HR / both, with quorum and delegation). Allow cancelling approved leave with a reason that re-credits the balance. Add *Approve* / *Refuse* for line managers.
4. **Recruitment step 2: careers API, candidate emails, interviews.** Publish openings through the public API (as Sites and Shop do) with an application form and CV upload into the applicant, parsed by "Fill from a document". Add stage email templates and refuse reasons with templates (English and Bangla). Book interviews in Agenda through `recruitment_calendar`, with each interviewer's scorecard tied to the meeting.
5. **Onboarding / offboarding plans and the departure flow.** Activity plans (steps per role with due-date offsets) launched on hire, from Hiring's one-step hire, and on departure. The departure flow records the reason and date, cancels future leave and allocations, archives the user, lists assigned equipment and vehicles to return, and hands the final-settlement inputs (unused earned leave, notice) to payroll.
6. **Skills, certifications and resume.** Skill types and levels, employee skills with history, certifications with expiry reminders, job-required skills, and a skills matrix pivot. Resume lines generated from contract and position history, a printable CV, and courses and events from Event Hub. Reuse the same skills for applicant matching in Hiring.
7. **Performance appraisals (beyond Odoo Community).** Review cycles with goals, self, manager and 360 feedback through structured forms (the scorecard pattern: independent until revealed), calibration, and outcomes feeding the contract's wage version and payroll increments. Odoo keeps this in Enterprise, so it is a clear lead for Kosh.
8. **Spend completeness.** Add foreign-currency expenses with the company rate on the date, split expenses, cost centres per expense (carried to `expense_account`), receipts by email alias and phone capture, line-manager approval through the approvals engine, and reimbursement through the next payroll run. Fleet (vehicles, drivers, contracts, odometer, costs, linked to Asset Care and mileage expenses) is the next larger item after these eight.
